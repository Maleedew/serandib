Serendib Trails is a small planning desk based in Colombo. We're named after the old word for this island — the one that gave English the word "serendipity" — because that's still the best way to describe how most good trips here actually happen.

We don't run buses of forty people between photo stops. Every itinerary on this site is built by someone who has actually driven the route, and every driver-guide we work with has been doing this for years, not weeks. If you'd rather plan it yourself, the map below has everything you need. If you'd rather hand it to someone, that's what the four steps further down are for.
